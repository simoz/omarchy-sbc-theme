# Media credits

The MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the theme files, not the
wallpaper artwork. Redistribution permission for all four wallpapers is
**pending**: they are derived from the Sunday Builders Club banner, original
artwork by [@ncfrontiersman](https://x.com/ncfrontiersman), and show the
club's name, so confirm with the artist and the Sunday Builders Club before
listing this theme publicly or submitting it to the Theme Registry.

## Wallpapers

- backgrounds/sunday-omarchy-con-scritta-4k.webp
- backgrounds/sunday-omarchy-senza-scritta-4k.webp
- backgrounds/sunday-retro-con-scritta-4k.webp
- backgrounds/sunday-retro-senza-scritta-4k.webp

Shared record for all four files:

- Creator: generated for the repository owner with an AI image generator
  (recorded in the owner's generation notes as "Built-in image_gen")
- Source: the Sunday Builders Club banner, original artwork by
  @ncfrontiersman (https://x.com/ncfrontiersman); X profile banner of
  https://x.com/SundayBuildClub,
  https://pbs.twimg.com/profile_banners/2111177022512705536/1792246203/1500x500,
  redrawn and corrected by the generator
- Process: generated at 1672×941, then resampled with macOS `sips` to
  3840×2160, then re-encoded from PNG to WebP (quality 95) with ImageMagick
  to keep the download small. This is not native 4K generation
- Style: the "omarchy" pair is an artistic interpretation of Omarchy Quattro's
  Tokyo Night landscape wallpapers (Winding Road, Sunset Lake); it is not an
  official Omarchy asset and contains none of their artwork. The "retro" pair
  follows the banner's sunburst
- Lettering ("con-scritta" files): "SUNDAY BUILDERS CLUB", the club's name
- Licence: not covered by the repository's MIT licence; the original artwork
  is @ncfrontiersman's
- Redistribution permission: pending (see above)
